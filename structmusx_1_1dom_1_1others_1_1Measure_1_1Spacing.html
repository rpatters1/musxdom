Detailed Description

A measure's horizontal spacing as Finale uses it to place metric positions.

The music area runs from options::MusicSpacingOptions::musFront plus frontSpaceExtra to width less options::MusicSpacingOptions::musBack. With a BeatChartElement array, each element is a slot, and the chart is laid out at whichever is wider, its ideal or its minimum positions. When BeatChartElement::Control::minWidth exceeds BeatChartElement::Control::totalWidth, each slot is at the first element's BeatChartElement::pos plus the element's BeatChartElement::minPos, and the spacing ends at the first element's position plus the minimum width. Otherwise each slot is at its own position plus the first element's minimum position, and the spacing ends at the total width plus that minimum position. A manually edited chart (minimum width 1) and one from before Finale 3.0 (no minimum width) always use their positions. Without a beat chart there is one slot, at the start of the music area.

Finale stretches the spacing uniformly to fill the music area, so a slot is drawn at musicStart plus its offset times musicWidth / spacingWidth. Either width can be zero or negative in a malformed or degenerate measure.
